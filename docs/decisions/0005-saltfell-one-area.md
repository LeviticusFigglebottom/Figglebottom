# ADR 0005: Saltfell is one area on one terrain

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-07
- **Implements:** ADR 0003 for the vertical slice

## Context

`docs/lore/SLICE.md` names three areas: `saltfell_strand`, `saltfell_steading` (the yard) and `saltfell_longhouse`. ADR 0003 asks for continuous terrain at area seams, a loop back to a Hearthstone opened from the far side, a vista, and route changes between THAW and RIME.

## Options

1. Three linked areas on one shared heightmap. Each area would build its own slice of the land, joined by portals at the cliff path and the Longhouse door.
2. One area, `saltfell_steading`, holding the whole sea-cliff landscape. The Strand, the yard and the Longhouse are named zones inside it.

## Decision

**Option 2: one area.** The slice is small: about 170 × 200 m of playable land, which builds and bakes its navmesh in about 1.5 s. If it were split, the boat-shed shortcut (yard → gully → boat-shed back door) and the cliff path would each cross an area seam. Every seam would then need a portal, a load and duplicated land, and that is exactly the loading-corridor feel ADR 0003 forbids. With one area, both routes are plain walking, the navmesh covers both of them, and the tests can measure the loop directly.

The slice's area ids are kept as **zone names** (`zones` in the area JSON: THE STRAND, SALTFELL, THE LONGHOUSE) for title cards and the journal. Hearth ids follow SLICE.md: `hs_boat_shed` (the Strand) and `hs_salt_house` (the yard). `hs_long_hearth` waits on the release beat.

## Layout (the route)

- **The Strand:** a shingle cove between two headlands, with sea to the south. The boat-shed stands at the cliff foot and holds the Boat-Shed hearth. The tidal channel runs out to the sea stack.
- **The cliff path:** three switchback legs cut into the west cliff face, joined by two turning landings. The walls between the legs are steep, and a drop from one leg to the next costs health.
- **Saltfell yard:** the turf shelf at about 22 m. It holds the Longhouse to the north, the byre, the store-house, drying racks, the well, salt pans and the Salt House hearth at the top of the path. The graves sit to the east, and dry-stone dykes follow the contours.
- **The Longhouse:** 12 × 32 m, with its door to the south. In RIME the frost veil stands in the doorway, and the Netmender waits at the high end.
- **The loop:** a gully cleft runs from the yard down to the boat-shed's back door. The door is barred inside the shed and can be unbarred only from the gully side. Once it is open, the yard is about 50 m from the Boat-Shed hearth instead of about 200 m.
- **RIME route:** the tidal channel freezes, and the player can walk to the sea stack for bog-iron. In THAW the channel is deep water, and the player drowns.
- **Vista:** the Lowe barrow hills rise to the north, across a lethal ravine. They can be seen from the yard but not reached.
- **Bounds:** sea and sea cliffs to the south and west, the ravine to the north, a scree fell (over 45°) to the east, and buildings' own walls. `tools/audit_edges.py` classes every walkable edge and fails on any it cannot class.

## Consequences

- The land is generated by `tools/terrain_saltfell.py`, a pure-Python script that writes `data/terrain/*.bin`. `tools/gen_steading.py` regenerates the terrain first and seats everything on it.
- A later region, the Lowe, can be its own area. Its seam with Saltfell is the ravine, and the crossing (a rope bridge to lower) is a portal on continuous terrain.
- If the slice grows past about 300 × 300 m, revisit this decision. Option 1 then becomes the way to keep build times down.
